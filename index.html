Inner Course

Where Moving Truth moves outward, Inner Course moves in.

Coming soon to iPhone Open the first page

scroll to begin

Not a feed. Not a quote collection. A companion.

Inner Course exists to create moments where a person pauses long enough to think. Its lessons do not rush toward answers. They invite reflection, writing, silence, return—and the kind of understanding that can become compassionate action.

Progress is measured by reflection, not completion.

⌁ Every lesson has a rhythm

A structure that makes room for meaning.

Each lesson follows the same deliberate path. It may end on a question. It may end in stillness. Nothing forces the reader onward before they are ready.

  1. Invitation
  2. Exploration
  3. Reflection
  4. Integration
  5. Stillness
The design compass

Is this truer?

Not: is it more exciting, more modern, or more impressive? Every uncertain decision in Inner Course returns to one question. Truth becomes the compass. Structure, not noise.

Reflection before reactionThe work values what is noticed and written, not how quickly a lesson is consumed.
Return, not finishingA quiet bookmark suggests that the path remains open whenever you are ready.
Craftsmanship, not spectaclePurposeful motion, tactile warmth, and restraint rather than attention-seeking effects.
Questions, not prescriptionsLessons help people ask better questions without pretending to hold every answer.
The book

Not new. Not neglected. Loved.

The leather-bound book is more than an icon. It is the cover of the first book every person opens: softened at the corners, marked by return, and waiting without urgency. The first screen should feel like turning its first page.

The bookmark stays. Someone has already walked this path. You are welcome to continue.

Fourteen doors

Every lesson opens through fourteen doors.

Inside the app, every lesson is being built in English, French, Spanish, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Hindi, Arabic, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, and Thai from the beginning—not added after the English experience is finished.

  • Translate the lesson, not merely its words.
  • Respect right-to-left reading and different ways language holds meaning.
  • Build an accessible common path without a separate, lesser experience.
  • Let quiet and dignity survive every translation.

A blessing for Inner Course

May every decision be made with care.
May every lesson invite honest reflection.
May every reflection become compassionate action.

Truth moves. Wisdom endures. Compassion multiplies.
